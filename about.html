1. Who We Are

XMActive is an independently operated informational website focused on providing clear, accurate, and up-to-date information about satellite radio and the the satellite radio service satellite radio platform. We are a small team of technology and media enthusiasts who are passionate about helping people get the most out of their satellite radio experience.

Our website was created to fill a gap in plain-language, easy-to-understand information about satellite radio — covering everything from how the technology works, to what channels are available, to which devices are compatible, and how subscription plans compare.

2. Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to be the most helpful, honest, and accessible independent guide to satellite radio on the internet. We believe that anyone should be able to find clear answers to their satellite radio questions without needing to read through technical manuals or wade through marketing materials.

We write every article and guide with the reader in mind — using plain language, logical structure, and real-world examples that make complex topics easy to understand.

5. Our Editorial Standards

We are committed to publishing accurate and reliable information. Our editorial standards include:

  • Fact-checking — all factual claims are verified before publication
  • Regular updates — we review and update content as the service and technology evolves
  • Clear sourcing — where we reference external information, we link to authoritative sources
  • Corrections — if we make an error, we correct it promptly and transparently
  • No clickbait — our headlines accurately reflect the content of our articles

If you spot an error or have a correction to suggest, please contact us and we'll review it as quickly as possible.
